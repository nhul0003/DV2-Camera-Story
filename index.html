The film era

For a century, cameras kept changing shape

Glass plates gave way to box cameras, then folding, roll-film and 35mm cameras. Each new type took over from the one before.

How to read: each row is a type of camera. Darker squares mean more surviving cameras from that decade. Hover for numbers; outlined squares are explained below.

Where Melbourne's cameras came from

Most cameras in Museums Victoria were made overseas, first in the USA, Britain and Germany, later in Japan. Eighteen were made here, mostly by Kodak in Melbourne.

How to read: each line runs from where cameras were made to Melbourne. Thicker lines and bigger circles mean more cameras. Drag across the years below the map to filter it.
