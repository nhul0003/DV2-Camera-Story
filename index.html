01

The film era

For a century, cameras kept changing shape

Glass plates gave way to box cameras, then folding, roll-film and 35mm cameras. Each new type took over from the one before.

Where Melbourne's cameras came from

Most cameras in Museums Victoria were made overseas, first in the USA, Britain and Germany, later in Japan. Eighteen were made here, mostly by Kodak in Melbourne.

02

The film industry collapses

Film cameras almost vanished in ten years

In 2000 the world exported US$3.6 billion of 35mm film cameras, over half from Hong Kong and China. By 2010 the trade had fallen by 96%.

How to read: each map is one year. Circles show each country's film camera exports, using the same scale on every map. The red ring marks Australia.

55% from Hong Kong and China

The last big year for film cameras. Hong Kong alone exported over US$1 billion worth.

2010 · down 96%
